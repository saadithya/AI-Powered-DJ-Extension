Privacy Policy

AI-Powered DJ Extension

Effective date: November 21, 2026
Last updated: November 21, 2026

In short: the extension has no server and no accounts. Your audio is processed inside your browser and is never recorded, stored, or uploaded. Your settings, presets, API keys, and license key are saved only on your own device. Data leaves the browser in exactly two situations, both started by you: an AI command you send to the provider you selected, and a Premium license check with Gumroad.

1. Who We Are

The AI-Powered DJ Extension is a browser extension that adds DJ-style audio controls — volume, equalizer, effects, turntable speed, sample pads, visual effects, and an optional AI command assistant — to videos on YouTube watch pages.

"We" refers to the developer of the extension. We do not operate any server, backend, database, or user account system. There is no sign-up, login, or profile.

2. Supported Sites

The extension runs only on YouTube video pages (youtube.com, www.youtube.com, m.youtube.com — paths containing /watch). It does not run on, and is not designed for, any other site. Spotify and SoundCloud are not supported in the current version.

3. Information Stored on Your Device

Everything below is written to Chrome's local extension storage (chrome.storage.local) on your own computer. It is not synced to your Google account and is not transmitted to us.

  • Audio settings and presets — volume, EQ, filter, effect values, turntable/disc mode, launchpad and visual-effect preferences, and any custom presets you create.
  • AI settings — which provider you selected (OpenAI, Anthropic, or Google), the on/off state of the AI assistant, and the API key(s) you entered.
  • Premium data — the Gumroad license key you entered, a Premium on/off flag, and the timestamp when your free AI trial started.

We cannot read this data. It lives inside your browser profile and is removed when you uninstall the extension.

Please note: API keys and the license key are stored as plain text in Chrome's local extension storage. Chrome does not encrypt this storage. Anyone with access to your computer or browser profile could read those values. Use a restricted API key with a spending limit, and do not reuse a key you rely on elsewhere.

4. Audio Processing

All audio manipulation (volume, equalizer, filters, effects, tempo/turntable changes, and sample playback) happens locally in your browser using the Web Audio API, applied to the video that is already playing on the page.

  • The extension does not record audio.
  • It does not store audio or save it to disk.
  • It does not upload audio anywhere.
  • It does not use your microphone, camera, screen, or tab-capture APIs.
  • It does not download or copy the video.

The bundled vocal samples and impulse responses are files shipped inside the extension itself; loading them is a local read, not a network request.

Data leaves your browser only in the two cases below. Both are initiated by you, and in both cases the request goes directly from your browser to that company. Nothing passes through a server controlled by us.

What is never included in an AI request: the page URL, video title, video ID, captions, page content, browsing history, your audio, or your settings. Only the command text you typed and a short fixed instruction block are sent. Only the one provider you selected is contacted — there is no automatic fallback to another provider and no escalation to a more expensive model.

Those companies process the request under their own terms and privacy policies, which we do not control. Their API usage is billed to your own account, separately from any Premium purchase. Avoid typing personal or sensitive information into the AI command box, since that text is sent to the provider you chose.

6. Information We Do Not Collect

  • No name, email address, phone number, or postal address.
  • No account, profile, or identifier that we could associate with you.
  • No browsing history, visited URLs, search terms, or page contents.
  • No analytics, telemetry, crash reporting, or usage statistics.
  • No advertising, ad identifiers, or third-party trackers.
  • No cookies set by the extension.
  • No audio, video, screenshots, microphone, camera, or location data.
  • No financial, health, government ID, or authentication credentials — other than the API keys and license key you voluntarily enter, which stay on your device.

8. Data Sharing and Sale

We do not sell, rent, trade, or otherwise share your data. We have no data to share: we never receive it. The only outbound transfers are the user-initiated requests in Section 5, sent directly by your browser to the provider or payment platform you chose to use.

9. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain nothing, because nothing reaches us. Locally stored data remains on your device until you remove it:

  • Clear individual items (for example an API key or license key) from the extension's settings panel.
  • Use the extension's reset option to restore default audio settings.
  • Uninstall the extension from chrome://extensions to delete all of its stored data.
  • Clearing your Chrome browsing data for "hosted app data" also removes extension storage.

Requests already delivered to an AI provider or to Gumroad are governed by that company's retention policy; contact them directly to exercise rights over that data.

10. Security

We reduce risk by keeping the design minimal: no server, no account, no analytics, and no audio upload. Network requests are made to fixed HTTPS endpoints over TLS. The extension does not execute remote code and loads only files packaged inside the extension.

No method of storage or transmission is perfectly secure. Because your keys are stored unencrypted in local extension storage, protect your device and browser profile, and revoke any key you believe has been exposed.

11. Children's Privacy

The extension is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children. If you believe a child has entered personal information into an AI command, delete that conversation from the provider's account and remove the stored API key from the extension.

12. Your Choices and Rights

  • AI is optional. Every manual control works without any API key or AI provider.
  • You choose the provider. Only the provider selected in the settings is contacted.
  • You can use your own key or none at all. Keys can be deleted at any time.
  • Local data is under your control and can be erased by uninstalling the extension.

13. International Users

Because we do not collect or receive data, there is no international transfer of data to us. When you send an AI command or verify a license, your browser transmits that request to the relevant company, which may process it in any country where it operates.

14. Changes to This Policy

If the extension's data practices change — for example, if support for additional sites or a new third-party service is added — this policy will be updated and the "Last updated" date above will change. Material changes will also be described in the extension's update notes.
